Get Random Brand Book

Brand book · version 1.0 · October 2026 · a Curioster project

Get random!

The identity, voice, assets and launch campaign for a compass that does not point north. Built on the original Curioster posts: the mustard, the terracotta, the condensed caps and the line “Have fun, be yourself and enjoy.”

1

Brand idea

Get lost on purpose.

The insight

Most of us live the same week fifty-two times a year. Routines are efficient, and they quietly flatten memory, mood and curiosity. People don’t lack ideas; they lack a gentle push that is specific, small and different every time.

The promise

Get Random hands you seven small doors a week, one a day, each grounded in motivation science and never repeated. You open them in your own way, write down what happened, and pass the best weeks on.

Personality

  • Curious, never random for its own sake. Every spark has a reason.
  • Warm, like a friend who texts “come on, try it”.
  • Brave, but small-brave. Nobody has to climb a mountain on a Tuesday.
  • Evidence-led, wearing its science lightly.
  • Open to everyone: any age, budget, body, city or culture.

Lines

  • Get lost on purpose. Campaign line.
  • Have fun, be yourself and enjoy. Heritage line from Curioster, kept as the sign-off.
  • Seven doors a week. Product descriptor.
  • Randomness, with a method. For press, partners and science audiences.
2

Logo

The bent needle.

  1. The ring is the week. A closed loop, like the seven days that repeat.
  2. Seven dots are the days. They sit inside the ring, evenly spaced, so no day is the default start.
  3. The needle bends. A compass needle that zig-zags instead of pointing north: the non-linear path.
  4. The dot is the “!”. Read the needle and its base together and you get the exclamation mark from the wordmark, tilted.
  5. The handle is heritage. Ring plus handle is the Curioster magnifying glass. Curiosity is still how you look.

Voice and accessibility

Talk like a curious friend.

Say

“Ask the barista one real question about their day.”

Specific, doable today, kind.

Don’t say

“Step outside your comfort zone and unlock your potential!”

Vague, preachy, hype.

Say

“Brief chats with strangers tend to lift mood. Sandstrom & Dunn, 2014.”

Science named, claim sized right.

Don’t say

“Science proves this will make you 40% happier.”

Over-claiming. We never invent numbers.

Writing rules

  • Sparks start with a verb and name a real thing.
  • One exclamation mark per piece, and the logo already has it.
  • No streak guilt, no “you missed a day”. Free days are allowed.
  • Plain words over jargon. Cite theories by name and year.
  • Inclusive by default: no assumptions about money, body, family, faith or city size.

Accessibility commitments

  • All text pairings meet WCAG AA; core text meets AAA.
  • Colour always paired with a shape and a word.
  • Every drag-and-drop has a keyboard Move menu.
  • Three text sizes built into the header.
  • Motion respects reduced-motion settings.
  • Light and dark themes designed, not inverted.

7

Campaign strategy

52 different weeks.

Objective

Turn the Curioster audience into the first Get Random community, then grow to people who feel their weeks blur together. Success is people who open doors, not people who download.

Core mechanic

Every Monday the brand publishes one public “week of the week”. Anyone can adopt it in the app with one tap, and share what happened behind each door. The campaign is the product, played in public.

Who it is for

The looped professional, 26–40

Same commute, same lunch, same scroll. Wants novelty that fits in 30 minutes.

The student in transition, 18–25

New city, few ties. Connect and Explore sparks are the hook; Commons is the social layer.

The newly free, 60+

Retired or kids moved out. Values Learn, Give and Happenings; needs big text and no jargon.

Four phases

Weeks −4 to 0

Seed

Invite Curioster followers into a closed beta. Run the original seven posts again with a “now it’s endless” reveal. Collect the first 50 diary stories (with consent) for launch content.

Weeks 1–2

Tease: the unmarked map

Posters and stories show only a dotted, zig-zag route and the line “Get lost on purpose.” A week code on each poster unlocks a week in the app.

Weeks 3–4

Launch: 52 different weeks

A public pledge: the brand lives 52 generated weeks and posts every door. Launch happening co-hosted with a local library or café, generated live on stage from the reels.

Week 5 onward

Sustain

Four content pillars on a weekly rhythm: Monday week drop, Wednesday community door, Friday Science in a minute, Sunday diary story. Monthly Commons “week of the month”.

Guardrails

  • No streak shaming, no loss-framed notifications.
  • Diary stays private; stories are only used with explicit opt-in.
  • Sparks are free or near-free and safe by design; no dares involving risk.
  • Clear note that Get Random is a curiosity tool, not therapy.
